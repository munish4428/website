Ph.D. Scholar

Manmohan Singh

His research focuses on Quantum Computing and Post-Quantum Cryptography, particularly code-based cryptographic schemes. His work addresses side-channel vulnerabilities and develops secure, implementation-aware solutions to ensure robustness of PQC systems in practical and resource-constrained environments.
