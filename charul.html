Ph.D. Scholar

KM Charul

Her research focuses on the integration of blockchain and Digital Twin technologies to develop secure, transparent, and resilient cyber-physical systems. The work emphasizes decentralized architectures, AI-driven analytics, and quantum-resistant security mechanisms to ensure data integrity, trustworthy communication, and system reliability. Contributions include both applied frameworks and scientometric analyses, with applications spanning healthcare, industrial systems, and sustainable resource management. The research aims to advance scalable and secure Digital Twin ecosystems through blockchain-enabled trust and automation.
