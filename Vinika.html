Ph.D. Scholar

Vinika Malik

Her research focuses on the integration of Digital Twin technology with advanced wireless communication networks to develop intelligent, adaptive, and resilient cyber-physical systems for the oil and gas industry. The work emphasizes real-time data acquisition, AI-driven predictive analytics, and Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) to ensure transparency, reliability, and informed decision-making. Contributions include both applied modeling frameworks and analytical studies, with applications in industrial monitoring, fault diagnosis, and operational optimization. The research aims to enhance efficient and trustworthy Digital Twin ecosystems through wireless connectivity, interpretable AI, and intelligent automation.
