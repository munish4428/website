Ph.D. Scholar

Rohit Kumar

His research focuses on Digital Twin technology for sustainable systems, particularly in smart agriculture and precision irrigation. His work integrates machine learning, deep learning, and reinforcement learning to enable intelligent, data-driven decision-making. He also explores scientometric analysis and interdisciplinary applications of Digital Twin across domains such as energy, healthcare, and transportation, emphasizing sustainability and real-world impact.
