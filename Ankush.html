Ph.D. Scholar

Ankush Manocha

His research interests include the development of intelligent smart monitoring frameworks using Internet of Things (IoT) and Digital Twin technologies. His work focuses on integrating deep learning and artificial intelligence techniques for predictive analytics and real-time decision-making. He is also interested in edge analytics, federated environments, and quantum-inspired computing to enhance system efficiency and scalability. Additionally, his research explores applications in smart healthcare, environmental monitoring, and sustainable agriculture. His contributions further extend to remote sensing, intelligent sensing systems, and cyber-physical system optimization. Overall, his research aims to design scalable, reliable, and data-driven intelligent systems for next-generation smart environments.
