About

Short Bio

Dr. Munish Bhatia is an Assistant Professor (Grade-I) at the National Institute of Technology Kurukshetra, India, with over 10 years of research experience in Internet of Things (IoT), artificial intelligence, digital twin technology, and cloud-fog computing. He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from Guru Nanak Dev University and previously served as a Project Engineer (Scientist) at IIT Mandi under the FarmerZone project. He has been recognized among the world's top 2% scientists by Stanford University for four consecutive years (2022-2025).

He has authored more than 120 Scopus-indexed publications, accumulating over 2300 citations with an h-index of 27, and has published in leading journals such as IEEE Internet of Things Journal, ACM Transactions, and Artificial Intelligence Review. His research focuses on secure and intelligent IoT systems, AI-driven predictive modeling, quantum-inspired optimization, and digital twin-enabled applications across healthcare, smart cities, and industrial systems.

Dr. Bhatia has secured multiple funded research projects from national and international agencies, including ANRF, Microsoft AI for Earth, and MeitY, and holds several granted patents in smart systems and healthcare technologies. He actively supervises Ph.D. and postgraduate students and serves as an Associate Editor of the IEEE Internet of Things Journal, contributing to the advancement of next-generation intelligent systems.
