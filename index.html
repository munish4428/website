Short bio

Dr. Munish Bhatia is an Assistant Professor (Grade-I) in the Department of Computer Applications at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Kurukshetra, India. His research focuses on Digital Twin systems, Internet of Things (IoT), Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing, and Cloud-Fog Computing, with an emphasis on intelligent and secure decision-making frameworks for real-world applications. He is recognized among the Top 2% Scientists worldwide by Stanford University (2022–2025). Dr. Bhatia has authored over 120 research publications, including more than 100 SCI/SCIE indexed journal papers, with a cumulative impact factor exceeding 693. His work has received over 2300 citations, with an h-index of 27. He has published extensively in leading venues such as IEEE, Elsevier, Springer, and ACM. Dr. Bhatia is actively involved in funded research projects supported by organizations such as Microsoft (AI for Earth), ANRF, and MeitY, focusing on areas including digital twin-driven industrial systems, AI-based environmental prediction, and quantum-inspired optimization. He is currently supervising multiple Ph.D. and postgraduate students and serves as an Associate Editor for reputed journals, contributing to the advancement of next-generation intelligent systems and sustainable technologies.
