About Me

Dr. Munish Bhatia is an Assistant Professor at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Kurukshetra, India, with a strong record of research excellence and academic contributions. He currently serves as the Principal Investigator (PI) of an ANRF-funded ARG MATRICS project, awarded a competitive research grant of INR 30 lakhs.

He is the holder of four granted Indian patents and has authored over 100 peer-reviewed SCI-indexed publications in leading international journals, including IEEE Transactions, ACM Transactions, and journals published by Wiley, Elsevier, and Springer. His scholarly work has achieved a cumulative impact factor exceeding 500, reflecting the quality and influence of his research.

Prior to joining NIT Kurukshetra, he worked as a Project Researcher at IIT Mandi under the Department of Biotechnology (DBT), Government of India. He also contributes to the global research community as an Associate Editor for journals such as IEEE Internet of Things and the Journal of Big Data (Springer), and serves as a reviewer for top publishers including Nature, IEEE, Elsevier, SAGE, Wiley, and Taylor & Francis.

Dr. Bhatia is a professional member of the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM), demonstrating his engagement with the broader computing research community.
