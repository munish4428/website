Short Bio

Dr. Munish Bhatia is an Assistant Professor (Grade-I) in the Department of Computer Applications at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Kurukshetra, India. His research focuses on Digital Twin systems, Internet of Things (IoT), Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing, and Cloud–Fog Computing, with emphasis on intelligent and secure decision-making frameworks for real-world applications.

He is recognized among the Top 2% Scientists worldwide by Stanford University (2022–2025). Dr. Bhatia has authored 120+ research publications, including 100+ SCI/SCIE indexed journal papers, with a cumulative impact factor exceeding 693. His work has received 2300+ citations, with an h-index of 27. His research appears in leading venues such as IEEE, Elsevier, Springer, and ACM.

He is actively involved in funded research projects supported by organizations such as Microsoft (AI for Earth), ANRF, and MeitY, focusing on digital twin-driven systems, AI-based environmental prediction, and quantum-inspired optimization. He currently supervises Ph.D. and postgraduate students and serves as an Associate Editor for reputed journals, contributing to the advancement of next-generation intelligent and sustainable technologies.
