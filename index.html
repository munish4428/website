Short bio

Dr. Munish Bhatia is an Assistant Professor at NIT Kurukshetra with strong contributions in Artificial Intelligence, Digital Twin, and IoT. He has published over 100 SCI-indexed papers and holds multiple patents.

His research focuses on AI-integrated Digital Twin systems, structural health monitoring, and smart infrastructure. He actively collaborates with global research communities and contributes as editor and reviewer in leading journals.
