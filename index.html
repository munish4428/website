Openings

PhDs and Postdocs: Dr. Munish Bhatia’s research group at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Kurukshetra is actively seeking highly motivated Ph.D. students, postgraduate researchers, and research interns interested in cutting-edge areas of computer science and intelligent systems.

How to Apply: Interested candidates are encouraged to send an updated CV, a Statement of Purpose or research interests, and academic transcripts (if available).

Short bio

Dr. Munish Bhatia is an Assistant Professor (Grade-I) at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Kurukshetra, India. His research interests include Digital Twin systems, IoT, Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing, and Cloud–Fog Computing.

He is recognized among the Top 2% Scientists worldwide by Stanford University (2022–2025). He has published over 120 research papers in leading journals including IEEE, Elsevier, Springer, and ACM, with more than 2300 citations and an h-index of 27.

His current research focuses on intelligent, secure, and scalable solutions for next-generation computing systems.
