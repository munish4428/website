Short bio

Dr. Munish Bhatia is an Assistant Professor at NIT Kurukshetra with strong research contributions in Artificial Intelligence, Digital Twin, and IoT.

He has published over 100 SCI-indexed papers, holds multiple patents, and actively contributes to leading journals as editor and reviewer.

His research focuses on AI-driven Digital Twin systems, structural health monitoring, and smart infrastructure.
