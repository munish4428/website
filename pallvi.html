Ph.D. Scholar

Pallvi

Her research focuses on the exploration and analysis of emerging technologies and their convergence within critical domains. Her research emphasizes the integration of advanced paradigms such as Digital Twin systems and next-generation communication networks (5G/6G) to enhance system efficiency, resilience, and real-time decision-making. She also contributes to scientometric investigations that map the evolution, trends, and impact of technological innovations, particularly in defense and high-reliability environments. Her work aims to provide structured insights into the strategic adoption of cutting-edge technologies for future-ready systems.
