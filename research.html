Academic Profile

Research Statement

Dr. Munish Bhatia’s research focuses on the development of intelligent, secure, and scalable computational frameworks for next-generation systems, with a strong emphasis on Digital Twin technologies, Internet of Things (IoT), Artificial Intelligence, and Cloud-Fog Computing.

📘 Research Overview

His work aims to bridge the gap between theoretical models and real-world industrial and societal applications. His research particularly explores AI-driven decision-making, secure data processing, and predictive analytics in cyber-physical systems. A significant portion of his work is dedicated to Digital Twin-based modeling and simulation, enabling real-time monitoring, optimization, and intelligent control of complex infrastructures such as smart cities, healthcare systems, and industrial environments.

He is also actively working on quantum computing-inspired optimization techniques and game-theoretic models to enhance system efficiency, security, and resilience in distributed environments. His research integrates simulation tools, machine learning models, and scalable architectures to address emerging challenges in sustainable and intelligent computing.

With a strong publication record in leading journals such as IEEE, Elsevier, Springer, and ACM, Dr. Bhatia is committed to advancing data-driven, secure, and adaptive systems for future technologies including Industry 4.0, 5G/6G networks, and smart infrastructure.

Research Interests

  • Digital Twin Systems for Smart Infrastructure and Industry 4.0
  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning for IoT Applications
  • Secure Cloud-Fog and Edge Computing Architectures
  • Quantum Computing-inspired Optimization Techniques
  • Game-Theoretic Models for Cyber-Physical Systems
  • Data-driven Predictive Analytics for Smart Healthcare and Cities

Research Vision

The core vision of this research is to design adaptive, intelligent, and secure computational ecosystems that can respond to real-time challenges, optimize performance, and support sustainable innovation across industrial, urban, and healthcare domains.
